## Editorial corrections

- Progressive tax brackets alone do not reduce take-home pay, but benefit reductions can affect the overall outcome. Average rates are not universally strictly lower than marginal rates.
- The raise example uses taxable income and calculates $375.61 extra federal tax, leaving $1,624.39 before other deductions and benefit effects.
- Distinguish gross business revenue, eligible expenses and net profit. GST/HST tests use taxable supplies over one or four quarters, include associates and have exemptions/special cases; curriculum-based tutoring can be exempt.
- GST/HST credit became the Canada Groceries and Essentials Benefit (CGEB) in July 2026. Older bundled benefit names were aligned. Eligibility, student exclusions for CWB and conditional refund examples are qualified.
- Fully exempt qualifying scholarships are not automatically reported as taxable income on line 13010. Moving expenses require the qualifying move, 40 km test and eligible-income restrictions.
- Tuition transfers are current-year tuition amounts, not cash refunds, reduced by the student's required use. Prior-year carryforwards cannot be transferred.
- FHSA opening age depends on provincial contract law, carryforward is limited, and opening starts its maximum participation period. RRSP room has annual caps and pension adjustments.
- Payroll reference table now includes CPP caps and CPP2; numbers are scoped to 2026 and outside Quebec. Tax thresholds use taxable income.
- Refunds may include refundable credits; ordinary interest remains taxable without a T5; tax sheltering is not limited to TFSA/FHSA.
- Tax-return corrections follow a Notice of Assessment; late penalties are distinguished from interest and repeat-filing penalties.
