Founder · Budding entrepreneur
Grade 11 · Vancouver, BC

I started TaxIntuitive because I want the next generation of adults to be genuinely prepared for the real world. It began with a conversation with my dad, who is a tax specialist, about the gaps he sees every day.

TaxIntuitive is a friendly, plain-language place to learn taxes and personal finance through trivia, expert videos, simulators, and clear guides.
